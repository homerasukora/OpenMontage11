## Where this sits in the funnel

Video → profile → link in bio → site. The video does **one** job: make the
account look like it knows something. No token talk, no contract, no ticker on
screen, no "link in bio" in the voiceover. The only ask is `FOLLOW THE SIGNAL`.

Product content is the other 20% and it is a different post.
