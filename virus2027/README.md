## House rules for anything published here

Taken from the growth deck and the web style guide, and non-negotiable:

- **80% mystery, 20% product.** Lore posts and product posts are different posts.
- **Ask, never declare.** Every piece ends on an open question and on `2027`.
- **No promises.** No price, no returns, no urgency, no claim that anything will happen in 2027.
- **No real person is presented as a factual conspirator.** Redaction bars, no names in the read.
- **Every factual claim is sourced** before it ships, and the sources go in the pinned comment.
- **Concede in public.** Where the evidence is weak, the video says so — that is what "a mystery you can believe in" costs, and it is why the format survives its own comment section.
